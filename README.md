## Problem
Tokenized stocks today are minted by a single Authorised Participant, and only while the underlying market is open. When the market is closed, supply freezes. On Aug 30–31, 2026 the tokenized HIMS float was cornered and traded at +112% over the Friday close until minting reopened ([DeFi Prime](https://defiprime.com/tokenized-stock-float-squeeze)).
